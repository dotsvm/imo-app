# Hunch
Status: active
Social prediction-trading demo ("Outlook" is the repository's working name). Source of truth: `design/00-Design-System.html`; tokens live in `apps/web/src/app/globals.css`.

Midnight ink: panels #090d0b, surface #121519, raised #1e2129, active #252832, hairline dividers #1c1f25. Text #eeefea, secondary #c3c7cd, muted #969ca6.
Money is the color: positive #b5e6a1 (Yes, gains), coral #e4998c (No, losses, destructive) — always with ▲▼, +/−, or the word. Gold #e2c892 marks demo, pending, partial and low sample.
Primary buttons are the app's green #b5e6a1 with dark green words #183127 (hover #ccf5e1); secondary buttons are the raised surface with a hairline. Every button is fully rounded: pills for words, circles for icons. Back and Fade are a pair of buttons, each in its own side's color.
The app never creates markets: people share a take on an existing market ("Share your take", "Choose a market").
Typography: Geist for UI with tabular figures; Instrument Serif for display. Radii: 6 tags, 10 fields, 12 cards, 16 sheets, 20 drawer.
Icons: Phosphor — fill for objects and places, bold for carets, arrows, close, check and plus (`apps/web/src/components/icons.tsx`).
Voice: precise, curious, calm. Every fictional performance and trade is labeled as simulated.
